# Bangladesh Income Tax Filing Guide — Individuals (IT-11GA)

### [Rakibul Hassan](https://aiwithr.github.io/about/) with the help of OpenCode

A practical, checklist-driven guide for salaried individuals filing on **etaxnbr.gov.bd**.  
No personal data — safe to share with friends and colleagues.

> Forms & rules referenced: IT-11GA (2023), IT-10B (2023), IT-10BB (2023), Income Tax Act 2023, and Finance Act rates for Assessment Year (AY) 2026-27. Always confirm slab/rebate rates against the latest Finance Act before filing.

---

## 1. Key concepts (read first)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Income Year (IY)** | 1 July → 30 June (e.g., IY 2025-26 = 1-Jul-2025 to 30-Jun-2026) |
| **Assessment Year (AY)** | The year you file for that income (AY 2026-27 covers IY ended 30-Jun-2026) |
| **TDS** | Tax Deducted at Source (salary, bank interest, sanchayapatra, etc.) |
| **Challan** | Proof of tax paid (Mushak/e-challan); has Challan Amount vs Claimed Amount |
| **AIT** | Advance Income Tax (e.g., annual car AIT) |
| **Rebate** | Legal reduction of tax for qualifying investments (Schedule 5) |
| **Net Wealth** | Last year's closing wealth + this year's sources − expenses (IT-10B) |

**Typical individual deadline:** 30 November following the income year (extensions possible — check NBR).

---

## 2. What you need before you start (document kit)

Collect for the income year (1 Jul → 30 Jun):

1. **Salary tax certificate** from employer (shows basic, allowances, employer PF, exempt amount, slab tax)
2. **Salary TDS challans** (employer-provided list: challan no, date, challan amount, **claimed/tax amount**)
3. **Bank interest TDS certificates** (each bank's TAX CERTIFICATE for the year)
4. **Sanchayapatra / DPS statements** (interest earned + source tax/TDS)
5. **Car AIT challan** (paid usually Nov each year; claimed as advance tax)
6. **Rent agreement + receipts** (municipal tax, repair bills if claiming house-rent income)
7. **Investment proofs for rebate:** PF/RPF statement (self + employer), LIP premium receipts, DPS, sanchayapatra purchase, share/MF statements
8. **Bank statements** up to 30 June (to declare year-end balances)
9. **Last year's submitted return** (for net-wealth carry-forward and last-year tax figures)
10. **Asset list** (property, car, furniture, ornaments) with acquisition costs

## 3. Tax slabs — AY 2026-27 (individual, other than women/65+)

Verified against a filed computation for total income ≈ 1.06 crore:

| Cumulative income up to | Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 4,00,000 | 0% |
| 4,00,001 – 7,00,000 | 10% |
| 7,00,001 – 11,00,000 | 15% |
| 11,00,001 – 16,00,000 | 20% |
| 16,00,001 – 36,00,000 | 25% |
| Above 36,00,000 | 30% |

**Quick method:**  
Tax = 0 + 30,000 + 60,000 + 1,00,000 + 5,00,000 + 30% × (Total Income − 36,00,000)  
*(for income above 36 lakh; sum the applicable bands only)*

**Minimum tax:** generally Tk 5,000 for individuals in city-corporation areas (payable = higher of slab tax after rebate, or minimum).

*Slabs change with Finance Acts — verify each year. Slightly different breakpoints applied in earlier years (e.g., nil band up to 3.5L / 4.5L historically).*

## 4. Income heads — what goes where (IT-11GA main page)

| Line | Head | Source / tip |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Employment (Schedule 1)** | Salary certificate: total received − exempt = taxable |
| 2 | **Rent (Schedule 2)** | House-rent received in the year (landlord side) |
| 3 | Agriculture | Usually 0 for city dwellers |
| 4 | Business | Professional/business income if any |
| 5 | Capital gain | Asset sale gains |
| 6 | **Financial assets** | Bank interest + sanchayapatra/dividend/MF profit (**gross, before TDS**) |
| 7 | Other sources | Royalty, honorarium, freelance, etc. |
| 11 | **Total income** | Sum of 1–10 |

### Schedule 1 — Employment (non-government pay scale, part b)

```
Basic pay
+ Allowances
+ Employer's RPF/PF contribution (line 11 — also an investment for rebate!)
+ Other receipts
= Total Salary Received (line 13)
− Exempt amount as per 6th Schedule Part 1 (line 14)
= Total Income from Salary (line 15)
```

**Nitty-gritty:**  
- Employer's PF contribution is **both** taxable-structurally (in salary total) **and** rebatable under Schedule 5 (see §6).  
- Exempt amount must match the employer certificate / 6th Schedule (don't invent it).

### Schedule 2 — Rent (you receive house rent)

```
1. Rent received / annual value
6. = Total Rental Value
7. Allowable deductions:
   (a) Repair & collection — commonly up to 25% of rent
   (b) Municipal / local tax (actual, with receipt)
   (c) Land revenue
   (d) Interest on loan for the property
   (e) Insurance premium
   (f) Other admissible expenses
8. = Total admissible deduction   ← MUST equal sum of (a)–(f)
9. Net income (6 − 8)            ← must equal 6 minus 8
```

**Critical check:** Line 7 total, line 8 total, and line 9 arithmetic must all agree. A mismatch (line 7 ≠ line 8) is a red flag that will fail scrutiny.

**Tip:** Exclude non-rent items bundled in transfers (e.g., car-parking charges credited alongside rent) or declare them honestly under the right head — trace every credit in your bank statement.

### Line 6 — Financial assets income

Include **gross interest** (before TDS) from:
- Every bank account (savings/FC)
- Sanchayapatra, DPS, PF interest if taxable
- Dividends, mutual funds

**Nitty-gritty:** Sum of all banks' "Interest Credited" totals + all sanchayapatra "Interest" figures. Do **not** miss a second/third bank — each bank's TDS also goes in the TDS annexure.

## 5. Tax computation page

```
12. Gross tax on total income (use §3 slabs)
13. Tax rebate + incentives (Schedule 5 + advance-tax incentive if any)
14. Net tax (12 − 13)
15. Minimum tax
16. Tax payable = higher of (14, 15)
17. Surcharge (net-wealth surcharge if wealth very high; usually 0)
18. Delay interest / penalty (if late or prior-year dues)
19. Total amount payable (16+17+18)
```

### Tax payment side

```
20. Total TDS claimed (salary + all banks + sanchayapatra + other)
21. Advance tax paid (car AIT, quarterly advance tax)
22. Refund adjustments (if any)
23. Tax paid WITH this return (fresh e-challan for the balance)
24. Total paid & adjusted (20+21+22+23)
25. Excess payment (24 − 19) → refund if positive; else you still owe
```

**Golden rule:**  
If **19 > (20+21)** → pay the difference via e-challan and put it in **line 23** before submit.  
Never leave line 23 at 0 while 19 exceeds TDS — the return won't be balanced.

## 6. Schedule 5 — Investment tax rebate (where you save the most)

### Rebate formula (Section 78, Income Tax Act 2023)

```
Rebate = LEAST of:
  (a) R% of qualifying investment     ← R = 10% for AY 2026-27 (15% in IY 2024-25 filings)
  (b) 3% of total taxable income      ← usually the binding cap for large investors
  (c) Tk 7,50,000                     ← lowered from Tk 10,00,000 by the Finance Act 2026
```

**Practical shortcut:** once `investment ≥ (3% of income) / R%` (≈ 30% of income at R=10%, ≈ 20% at the old R=15%), extra investment does **not** increase the rebate — line 13 is stuck at **3% of total income**.

*(The Finance Act 2026 cut the rebate rate from 15% to 10% and the ceiling from Tk 10 lakh to Tk 7.5 lakh for AY 2026-27 — employer certificates and older blogs still quote the old numbers, and the e-Return portal computed the new 10%. Always re-check line 13 after filling Schedule 5 — don’t hardcode an old rate. Deep-dive + sources: [tax_optimize.md](tax_optimize.md).)*

### Where to put each investment (wrong serial = lost rebate)

| Sch-5 # | Instrument | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Life insurance premium | — |
| 2 | Deposit Pension Scheme (DPS) | — |
| 3 | **Govt securities, sanchayapatra**, unit certificates, MF, ETF | Leaving blank while holding sanchayapatra |
| 4 | Listed shares / mutual funds | — |
| 5 | PF under PF Act 1925 (specific private PFs) | Putting **RPF** here by mistake |
| 6 | **Self + Employer contribution to RPF** | Claiming only employer's half |
| 7–9 | Superannuation / benevolent / zakat | — |

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Line 6 of Schedule 5 = **self contribution + employer contribution** (both!)
- [ ] Sanchayapatra purchases/balance in **Sl.3** (not zero if you hold any)
- [ ] Total investment (line 11) = sum of lines 1–10
- [ ] Rebate (line 12) matches line 13 on the main tax page
- [ ] min(R% × investment, 3% × income, 7,50,000) ≈ rebate (AY 2026-27: R=10% — confirm rate & cap on the portal yearly)

**Worked example (illustrative, not personal):**  
Total income 60,00,000 · Investment (RPF both + sanchay + DPS) = 8,00,000  
Rebate = least(10%×8L = 80,000 · 3%×60L = 1,80,000 · 7,50,000) = **80,000** (AY 2026-27 rates)

## 7. TDS annexure (Source Tax, AIT & Regular Tax Claim)

### Salary challans

For each challan enter **both** columns correctly:

| Challan Amount | Claimed Amount |
|---|---|
| Total tax deposited on that challan (e.g., company-wide or as printed) | **Your tax portion only** as per employer certificate |

**Classic error:** copying the full Challan Amount into Claimed Amount → massively overstates TDS → false refund claim → scrutiny. Always tie Claimed Amount total to your employer's "Total tax deducted" figure.

### Bank TDS

One row per account: interest credited (gross) + TDS deducted. Get a tax certificate from **each** bank — not just the main one.

### Sanchayapatra TDS

Certificate-wise: registration no, issue date, value, interest, source tax.

### Car AIT / advance tax

List under advance tax (**line 21**), with its own annexure row (challan no, date, vehicle reg, claimed amount). Challan date must fall in the relevant payment cycle (typically November for car AIT).

**Never** park car AIT in line 13 as a “rebate/incentive” — that double-counts or drops the credit. Line 13 is Schedule 5 (+ only a genuine first-quarter advance-tax incentive the portal itself adds).

### Where the claims actually come from: Update Tax Payment Status → eReturn Ledger

On the Tax & Payment page, **Update Tax Payment Status** opens a second portal (`ledger.etaxnbr.gov.bd`) with your session carried over. That ledger is the machine-readable backing for lines 20/21/23:

| Ledger menu | Backs | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Claim Source Tax → Salary (iBAS++ / Others) | line 20 salary part | list of employer challans, **Claimed Amount = your slice** |
| Claim Source Tax → Bank/FI, Sanchayapatra, … | line 20 rest | rows appear as Verified |
| Claim AIT → **AIT on Car** | line 21 | search by **Unique Key (Transaction No.)** of the AIT challan |
| **Tax Paid with Return (173)** | line 23 | after you pay the balance: enter Challan No → Save |
| Tax Payment Status (dashboard) | sanity | totals should mirror line 20 + 21 (+23) |

- Claims already reflected on the return → **leave them alone** (the ledger blocks duplicates: *“Claim already exists …”*).
- Line 23 stays 0 until the s.173 challan is entered — pay first, then claim, then submit.

**Final tie-out before submit:**
```
Sum(Claimed salary TDS) + Sum(bank TDS) + Sum(sanchay TDS) = Line 20
Line 20 + 21 + 22 + 23 = Line 24
Line 24 − Line 19 = Line 25 (or 0)
Ledger dashboard total = Line 24
```

## 8. IT-10B — Assets & Liabilities (mandatory if any trigger applies)

**Must file if:** total assets > Tk 50,00,000 · own a motor car · house/apartment in city-corporation area · foreign assets · company shareholder/director.

### The balancing identity (this is where most drafts fail)

```
Line 1  Sources of fund      = Total income + tax-exempt income + gifts
Line 2  Previous year net wealth  = LAST YEAR's Line 5 (closing) — copy exactly
Line 3  = 1 + 2
Line 4  Expenses (IT-10BB lifestyle + other)
Line 5  This year net wealth = 3 − 4
Line 7  Gross wealth         = 5 + liabilities
Line 10 Total assets         = 7  ← MUST EQUAL gross wealth
```

**Hard checks:**
- [ ] Opening wealth (line 2) **identical** to last year's submitted line 5
- [ ] Property "start value" this year = "end value" last year
- [ ] Movement during year (increase/decrease column) explains any change
- [ ] Sanchayapatra roll-forward: last balance − matured + purchased = this balance
- [ ] Bank balances = 30 June statements (all accounts, not zero)
- [ ] Cash in hand declared if material
- [ ] Line 8 asset items sum = line 10 = line 7

### Property particulars table

| Description | Start cost | Increase/Decrease | End cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flat/land (with sq ft / decimal) | last year's end | purchases/sales this year | start ± change |

**Nitty-gritty:** Never change start values silently; never leave "0 sq ft" or blank sizes; joint property → note ownership share.

## 9. IT-10BB — Lifestyle expenses statement

| # | Head | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Food, clothing, essentials | Realistic annual family spend |
| 2 | Housing (rent/maintenance) | Rent paid, society charges |
| 3 | Transport | Fuel, servicing, driver, tickets |
| 4 | Utilities | Electricity, gas, water, internet, mobile |
| 5 | Education | School fees, courses, books |
| 6 | Travel / vacation | Domestic + foreign trips |
| 7 | Festival / special / medical / other | Big-ticket items with comments |
| 8 | **TDS + last year's tax** (incl. sanchay TS) | Label asks for current-year TDS (with sanchay source tax) **and** tax paid as per last year’s return. Leaving 0 while income is ~1 Cr looks wrong; a defensible simple build = this-year TDS + last-year balance challan + car AIT (document your choice in a note). |
| 9 | Loan interest | Personal loan interest |

**Total must flow into IT-10B line 4.**  
Expenses should be defensible with bills; lifestyle far below income raises questions, far above needs explanation (gifts/savings drawn).

## 10. End-to-end filing flow (live portal map)

1. **Login** etaxnbr.gov.bd → eReturn tile → “I am already registered” → TIN + password  
2. The **Regular e-Return wizard** opens (existing draft auto-resumes):  
   **Assessment info → Additional information → Income** (Employment · Financial Assets · Rent · Tax Exempted) → **Rebate (Sch-5)** → **Expenditure (IT-10BB)** → **Assets & Liabilities (IT-10B)** → **Tax & Payment** → **Return View**  
   (top tab bar + sub-nav; every section has Back / Save Draft / Save & Continue)
3. Verify pre-filled TIN, name, employer, circle/zone (Assessment step)
4. Patch **Employment (Sch-1)** from salary certificate; **Rent (Sch-2)** cross-foot 7/8/9  
5. **Financial assets** income (banks + sanchay gross) → line 6  
6. **Rebate (Sch-5)** investments (`Import from Income` helper) → line 13  
7. **IT-10BB** lifestyle rows + line-8 tax row; **IT-10B** wealth (`Import and Autofill`)  
8. **Tax & Payment**: computation auto-fills; verify lines 20–21 against the ledger  
9. **Update Tax Payment Status** → ledger: verify claims; balance due → pay e-challan → claim in **s.173**  
10. **Return View** — read the whole form (English/Bangla), run the §11 checklist  
11. Human clicks **Submit Return** (only button of its kind, only on Return View)  
12. Download/print acknowledgment + submitted PDF; keep challans & certificates 5–6 years

## 11. Pre-submit verification checklist (copy this)

### Main page
- [ ] Line 11 = sum of heads 1–10
- [ ] Slab tax on line 11 recomputed by hand ≈ line 12
- [ ] Line 13 = Schedule 5 line 12 (+ incentive if any)
- [ ] Line 16 = max(line 14, 15)
- [ ] Line 19 = 16+17+18

### Payments
- [ ] Every challan's **Claimed Amount** = your tax slice (not full challan value)
- [ ] Line 20 = Σ claimed(salary) + Σ bank TDS + Σ sanchay TDS
- [ ] Car AIT in line 21 (if paid)
- [ ] Line 23 = balance challan if 19 > (20+21)
- [ ] Line 24 = 19 (excess 0) unless genuine refund
- [ ] Refund (line 25 > 0) only when truly overpaid — never from inflated TDS

### Schedules
- [ ] Schedule 1 ties to employer certificate
- [ ] Schedule 2: line 7 = Σ(a–f); line 9 = 6 − 8
- [ ] Schedule 5: correct serials; both PF halves; sanchay in Sl.3; line 11 sums
- [ ] Exempt income (line 26) = Schedule 1 exempt + other exemptions = sources-of-fund attachment

### IT-10B / IT-10BB
- [ ] Opening wealth = last year's closing wealth (exact)
- [ ] Property starts = last year ends; movements explained
- [ ] Sanchay roll-forward correct
- [ ] Bank balances from 30 June statements (every account)
- [ ] **Total assets = gross wealth (line 10 = line 7)**
- [ ] IT-10BB total = IT-10B line 4
- [ ] No blank sizes/shares on joint assets

### Common failure patterns to avoid
1. Claiming full challan amount as TDS  
2. Rebate in wrong Schedule-5 row; ignoring self PF; sanchay left at 0  
3. Schedule 2 internal add mismatch  
4. Forgetting a second bank's interest/TDS  
5. Opening net wealth ≠ last year's closing  
6. Assets ≠ wealth (bank/cash left blank at 0)  
7. Line 23 = 0 while tax still due  
8. Line 13 ≠ Schedule 5 rebate  
9. Property movement column 0 while values changed  
10. Lifestyle line 8 = 0 despite sanchay TS / last-year tax

## 12. Tax-saving playbook (legal levers)

*Agent helper: `python tools/optimize.py` (runbook Phase 2a) generates this analysis — slab position, rebate-cap verdict, Schedule-5 headroom — for your own numbers, in English + Bangla.*

### A. Schedule 5 investments (direct rebate — effective R% back, capped at 3% of income)

Priority order (typical):

| Instrument | Notes |
|---|---|
| **RPF — claim BOTH self & employer** | Often missed; employer already appears in Sch-1 line 11 |
| **Sanchayapatra** | Govt bonds; buy before 30 June to count for the year; Sl.3 |
| **DPS** | Systematic; Sl.2 |
| **Life insurance premium** | Premium paid in year; Sl.1 (sum-assured rules apply) |
| **Mutual funds / ETF / unit certificates** | Sl.3 |
| **Listed shares** | Sl.4 |
| **Zakat** | Sl.9 |

**Target:** invest until `R% × investment ≥ 3% × total income` (rebate then hits the 3%-of-income ceiling) — beyond that extra investment still builds wealth but doesn't cut tax further via rebate.

**Year-end timing:** complete investments **before 30 June** (income-year end). A cheque on 29 June counts; 1 July does not.

### B. Structure income & deductions

- **Rent received:** claim full admissible deductions (repair 25%, municipal tax, insurance, loan interest) with receipts — under-claiming inflates tax.
- **Exempt income:** ensure pension/gratuity/6th-schedule exemptions are claimed in line 26 (they also feed sources-of-fund).
- **Advance tax incentive:** paying Q1 advance tax on time can add a small incentive to line 13 — don't skip if your TDS falls short early in the year.
- **Employer tax planning:** if TDS is low vs actual liability, pay voluntary advance tax quarterly to avoid lump-sum March pressure + interest.

### C. Record discipline (saves tax + saves notices)

- One folder per year: challans, certificates, statements, bills
- Tie every number in the return to a document **before** filing
- Keep last year's submitted PDF — it is the source for this year's opening wealth, prior tax paid, and consistency

### D. What **not** to do

- Don't inflate investments without proof (verification risk)
- Don't show bank balance 0 when accounts exist (NBR receives bank reports)
- Don't claim refunds by overstating TDS
- Don't leave assets vs wealth gap "for later" — fix before submit
- Don't file with draft watermarks / incomplete annexures

## 13. Quick reference — reconciliation formulas

```
Salary taxable      = Total received − 6th Schedule exempt
Rent net            = Rental value − admissible deductions
Total income        = Σ all heads
Gross tax           = slab computation on total income
Rebate              = min(R% investment, 3% total income, 7,50,000)   [AY 2026-27: R=10%, cap 7.5L — confirm yearly]
Tax payable         = max(gross tax − rebate, minimum tax)
Line 20 TDS         = salary claims + all bank TDS + sanchay TDS
Balance due         = tax payable − (TDS + advance tax)  → pay via line 23
Closing net wealth  = opening wealth + (taxable + exempt income + gifts) − expenses
IT-10B balance      = Total assets must equal Gross wealth
```

## 14. When to ask a professional

Consult a BTRN-authorized advisor or tax practitioner if you have:
- Business/professional income, capital gains, or foreign income
- Very high wealth or complex property transactions
- Prior-year amendments, appeals, or notices
- Uncertainty about exemptions or rebate caps
